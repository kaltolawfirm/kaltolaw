Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers to common legal inquiries.

What is the first step in a business dispute?

The first step is a comprehensive case evaluation to understand your objectives and review all relevant documentation. We will then advise you on the most strategic path forward, whether that involves negotiation, mediation, or immediate litigation.

How do you structure your consultation and fees?

We believe in transparent billing. Depending on the matter, we offer hourly rates, flat fees, or contingency arrangements. During our initial consultation, we will clearly outline the expected costs and fee structures.

Do you offer specialized services for startups and new companies?

Yes. We have a dedicated team for enterprise formation, compliance, and IP protection, ensuring your new business is built on a solid legal foundation from day one.

How do I schedule a consultation?

You can schedule a consultation by calling our office directly, emailing us, or clicking the "Book Consultation" button on our website. We offer both in-person and virtual meetings.
