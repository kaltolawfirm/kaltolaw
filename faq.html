General

What is the first step in a business dispute?

The first step is a comprehensive case evaluation to understand your objectives and review all relevant documentation. We then advise on the most strategic path — negotiation, mediation, or litigation.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Call us at +250 790 750 000, email info.blaw223@yahoo.com, or click "Book Consultation" on our website. We offer both in-person and virtual meetings.

Do you offer virtual / online consultations?

Yes. We offer consultations via phone and video call. Simply mention your preference when booking and our team will arrange the most convenient format for you.

How long does a typical case take?

Duration depends on complexity, area of law, and whether it proceeds to litigation or ADR. We provide a realistic timeline estimate specific to your matter during the initial consultation.

Fees & Billing

How do you structure your fees?

We offer hourly rates, flat fees, or contingency arrangements depending on the matter. All costs are clearly outlined before engagement — no surprises. Try our Fee Estimator for a guide.

Is the initial consultation free?

We offer a brief initial consultation to understand your matter and explain how we can help. Any extended consultation fees are clearly communicated in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, mobile money (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money), and cash at our office. Payment plans may be available for qualifying clients.

Corporate & Business

Do you offer services for startups and new companies?

Yes. We have a dedicated team for enterprise formation, compliance, and IP protection, ensuring your business is built on a solid legal foundation from day one.

Can you help with company registration in Rwanda?

Absolutely. We handle the complete registration process through the Rwanda Development Board (RDB), including documentation, shareholder agreements, and regulatory compliance.

Do you handle cross-border and international matters?

Yes. We have extensive experience with cross-border commercial transactions, international trade agreements, and multi-jurisdictional disputes, collaborating with partner firms across the region.

Family & Personal

How do you handle sensitive family matters?

We approach all family matters — divorce, custody, and asset division — with utmost discretion, sensitivity, and a focus on long-term stability for all parties, especially children.

What is estate planning and do I need it?

Estate planning prepares for the legal management and distribution of your assets. If you own property or have dependents, it is essential. We handle wills, trusts, and inheritance arrangements.
